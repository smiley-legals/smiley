Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Smiles Trivia Bot (“Bot”) processes when it is used on Discord.

Information processed

The Bot may process Discord server IDs and configured trivia-channel IDs to remember server settings; Discord user IDs when a user submits an answer or invokes an admin/owner command; question answers and interaction metadata needed to operate trivia; and technical error logs needed to diagnose failures. The Bot does not need your Discord password.

Why information is used

Information is used to provide trivia, track the active question and timeout, save server configuration, enforce command restrictions, and maintain reliability and security.

Storage and retention

Server settings are stored in a PostgreSQL database. Active-question state is held in memory while the Bot runs. Technical logs may be written to the Bot host. Data is retained only as needed to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the Bot, subject to the operator’s hosting and backup practices.

Sharing

The Bot does not sell personal information. Data may be processed by infrastructure providers used to host the Bot and database, or disclosed where required by law or necessary to protect the service and its users.

Your choices

You can stop interacting with the Bot or ask a server administrator to remove it. For questions or requests about data, contact the Bot operator through the support/contact method published with the Bot. Some technical records may remain in backups or logs for a limited period.

Children and third-party services

The Bot is operated on Discord and is subject to Discord’s own terms and privacy practices. Do not use the Bot in a way that violates Discord’s age requirements or applicable law.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as the Bot changes. Continued use after an update means the revised policy applies. For privacy questions, contact the Bot operator through the support/contact method published with the Bot.
